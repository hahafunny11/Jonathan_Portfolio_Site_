Glory For Goblins


Glory For Goblins is a real-time strategy game where you, the King Goblin, leads your army of goblins to burn down the human's homes to drive them out of your lands.
I am the lead level designer for GFG. Not only are two levels completely designed by me, with many level concepts being in the works, but I also cleaned up every single level to make them more vibrant and consistent with each other.
I've also done some programming work; designing a breakable bridge, a zone that zooms the game's camera out when the player is in it, and making a moving water texture that designers can easily create instances of and modify to create variants.
